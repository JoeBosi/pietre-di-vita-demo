Pietre di Vita: the story of an olive oil bottle

Goal: understand how an olive oil bottle is recorded, in which phases and with which tools, and how anyone can verify it. The stories and data are made up: Pietre di Vita is a fictional Tuscan mill.

Chapters 1-4 are in plain language, no code. Chapters 5-9 are for those who want to build a minimal version.
